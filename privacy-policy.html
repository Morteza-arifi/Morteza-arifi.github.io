Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-08

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application “Einbürgerungstest – LID” (the “App”), developed by Moredeveloper. The App is ad-supported and does not collect personal data directly from users.

1. Data Collection by Third Parties

The App uses Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements. These Google services may collect and process certain information from your device, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and technical information (device type, OS version, IP address)
  • Approximate location (when enabled for personalized ads)

All such data collection and processing is performed by Google and its partners according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Information

The App itself does not collect, store, or share personal information.

2. User Consent

In regions where required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App uses Google’s UMP consent framework to request user consent for personalized advertising. Users can manage or withdraw their consent through the privacy options presented within the App.

3. Intended Audience

The App is intended for users 16 years of age and older, as it is designed for individuals preparing for citizenship testing. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Data Retention

The App itself retains no personal data. Any data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s own policies.

5. Security

Because the App does not store personal information, no user data is maintained by the developer. Google and its partners apply industry-standard safeguards to protect any data they process.
